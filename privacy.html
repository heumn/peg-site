Privacy

Peg collects nothing.

Your workouts, ride history, and sensor pairings are stored only on your device. The app has no account system, no analytics, no ads, no third-party code, and no servers of its own — it never sends your data anywhere.

Bluetooth is used for one thing: talking to your trainer and heart rate monitor, locally.

If the app crashes, Apple may share an anonymous crash report with the developer — only if you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in iOS Settings.
